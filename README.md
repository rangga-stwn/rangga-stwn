### Featured Data Analytics Projects

#### 1. [ApexMart Retail — Sales & Profitability Analysis](https://github.com/rangga-stwn/apexmart-retail-analytics)
*End-to-end individual project analyzing 9,993 retail transactions (2014–2017) to uncover why profit margins declined despite steady revenue growth.*
- **Tools:** Excel, PostgreSQL, Power BI Desktop
- **What I did:** Cleaned and standardized 24 columns, wrote PostgreSQL queries (CTEs & `CASE` logic) to benchmark KPIs, and built a 2-page interactive Power BI dashboard with 7 DAX measures.
- **Key takeaway:** Identified a **30% discount loss threshold** and pinpointed profit leakage in specific Furniture sub-categories (`Tables` and `Bookcases`) and the `Central` region.

#### 2. [Melbourne Property Investment Analysis](https://github.com/rangga-stwn/melbourne-property-investment-analysis)
*Group capstone project (MSIB Kampus Merdeka x RevoU Tech Academy) analyzing 17,539 property records in Melbourne (2016–2017).*
- **Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI Desktop
- **What I did:** Served on the **Data Cleaning Team**—auditing 21 variables, standardizing formats, and handling missing numerical values via median/mean imputation before regression modeling and forecasting.
- **Key takeaway:** Mapped regional price growth leaders (`Eastern` +4.00% & `Southern` +3.51%), seasonal Q2–Q3 sales peaks, and a **+11.05% 5-year average ROI** for properties near the CBD.
